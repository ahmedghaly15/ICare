## Introduction

ICare is a comprehensive child care app that combines the power of AI and community support to assist mothers in taking care of their babies. From predicting the baby's needs to providing speech therapy and connecting with other mothers, ICare aims to make motherhood a little easier and a lot more joyful.

## AI-Assisted Features 🤖

### Baby Cry Predictor 🎤

Record a 10-second audio of your baby to determine if they are hungry, tired, or need something else. This feature helps you understand your baby's needs better and respond quickly.

### ICareBot 💬

Interact with our ICareBot using the Gemini API from Google. Ask questions, seek advice, and get instant responses to help you navigate motherhood with ease.

### SpeechTherapy 🗣️

Designed to help children with speech difficulties, this feature includes three levels of exercises. Each level has sub-levels where the child listens to an audio challenge, records their attempt, and receives feedback. A score above 50% allows them to progress to the next level.

## Features 🌟

### Medical 🏥

Access important medical information quickly:
- **Emergency**: Immediate steps and information for common emergencies.
- **Medical Information**: Details about regular diseases like vomiting, choking, etc., providing quick access to necessary information.

### Tips 💡

Receive a daily random parental advice to help you navigate the day-to-day challenges of motherhood.

### ICare Community 👩‍👩‍👧‍👦

Connect and communicate with other mothers:
- **Tiny Tales (Posts)**: Share your stories and baby pictures.
- **Comments and Replies**: Engage with other mothers' posts by commenting and replying.
- **Likes**: Show appreciation for posts, comments, and replies.
- **Bookmark**: Save posts for later.
- **Follow/Unfollow**: Build your network by following or unfollowing other mothers.
- **Chat**: Private messaging with other moms.

### Other Features 🔧

- **Authentication**: Secure login and registration.
- **Notifications**: Stay updated with the latest activities and messages.
- **Dark Theme**: A comfortable viewing experience for all lighting conditions.

We have implemented smooth animations and a friendly user interface to ensure a delightful user experience. Our goal is to make mothers feel happy and supported while using ICare.
